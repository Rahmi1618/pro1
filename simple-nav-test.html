Mobile Navigation Test

Resize your browser to mobile width and click the hamburger menu (3 lines) in the top-right corner.

Test Instructions:

  1. Make your browser window narrow (less than 1024px wide)
  2. Look for the 3 horizontal lines (hamburger menu) in the navigation
  3. Click the hamburger menu
  4. The menu should slide out and the hamburger should turn into an X
  5. Click a menu item or outside the menu to close it
