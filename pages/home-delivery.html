Delivery FAQ

What if I'm not home during delivery?

Our delivery personnel will call you before arrival. If you're not available, we can leave the medicine with a trusted neighbor, reschedule delivery, or arrange pickup from the pharmacy.

How do you ensure medicine quality during delivery?

We use temperature-controlled delivery bags for sensitive medicines, secure packaging to prevent damage, and trained personnel who understand proper handling procedures.

Can I track my delivery in real-time?

Yes! You can track your order through our website or receive SMS updates. You'll know exactly when your delivery is on the way and can see the estimated arrival time.

What payment methods are accepted for delivery?

We accept TeleBirr, bank transfers, and cash on delivery. Payment can be made online during ordering or to the delivery personnel upon receipt.
