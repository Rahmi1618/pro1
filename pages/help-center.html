Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

To create an account, click the "Login" button in the top navigation, then select "Create Account". Fill in your personal details including name, email, phone number, and create a secure password. You'll receive a confirmation email to verify your account.

How do I upload a prescription?

You can upload prescriptions on the Medicines page. Click the "Upload Prescription" area and select an image (JPG, PNG) or PDF file of your prescription. Our AI system will automatically extract the medicine information and help you find the best prices.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept TeleBirr (primary), bank transfers, major credit cards, and cash on delivery. All payments are processed securely with SSL encryption. TeleBirr payments are instant and don't have additional fees.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary by pharmacy and location. Most orders are delivered within 30-60 minutes in urban areas. You can track your order in real-time and receive notifications about delivery status.

How does the AI recommendation system work?

Our AI analyzes medicine availability, prices, pharmacy locations, and your preferences to recommend the best options. It considers factors like distance, price, stock levels, and pharmacy ratings to provide personalized suggestions.

Can I cancel or modify my order?

You can cancel orders that are in "Pending" or "Confirmed" status. Once an order is being processed or shipped, cancellation may not be possible. Contact the pharmacy directly or use our live chat for assistance with order modifications.
