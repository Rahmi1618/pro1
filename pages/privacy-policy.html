Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are our top priorities

Last Updated: January 1, 2024

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to PharmaCare, an AI-integrated pharmacy management system operated by Wollo University, Kombolcha Institute of Technology. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform, website, and services.

By using PharmaCare, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with the practices described in this policy, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Register for an account
  • Place orders for medicines
  • Upload prescriptions
  • Contact our support team
  • Subscribe to our newsletter
  • Participate in surveys or promotions

This information may include:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Delivery address and location data
  • Payment information (processed securely through third-party providers)
  • Medical prescriptions and health-related information
  • Communication preferences

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use our services, we automatically collect certain information:

  • Device information (IP address, browser type, operating system)
  • Usage data (pages visited, time spent, features used)
  • Location data (with your permission)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

2.3 Pharmacy Partner Information

For registered pharmacies, we collect:

  • Business information (name, license, address)
  • Inventory and pricing data
  • Order fulfillment information
  • Performance metrics and analytics

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

3.1 Service Provision

  • Process and fulfill medicine orders
  • Provide AI-powered medicine recommendations
  • Connect you with nearby pharmacies
  • Process payments securely
  • Coordinate delivery services

3.2 Communication

  • Send order confirmations and updates
  • Provide customer support
  • Send important service announcements
  • Deliver newsletters and promotional content (with consent)

3.3 Improvement and Analytics

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our services
  • Train and improve our AI algorithms
  • Conduct research and development
  • Generate anonymized analytics and reports

3.4 Legal and Safety

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect against fraud and abuse
  • Enforce our terms of service
  • Respond to legal requests

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

4.1 With Pharmacy Partners

We share necessary order information with pharmacies to fulfill your medicine orders, including:

  • Your name and contact information
  • Delivery address
  • Prescription details (when applicable)
  • Order specifications

4.2 With Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in:

  • Payment processing (TeleBirr, banks)
  • Delivery services
  • Cloud hosting and data storage
  • Analytics and performance monitoring
  • Customer support tools

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information when required by law or to:

  • Comply with legal processes
  • Respond to government requests
  • Protect our rights and property
  • Ensure user safety

5. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: All data transmission uses SSL/TLS encryption
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls and authentication
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Staff Training: Regular security training for all personnel
  • Incident Response: Procedures for handling security incidents

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

6.1 Access and Correction

  • View and update your account information
  • Request copies of your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information

6.2 Data Portability

  • Request your data in a portable format
  • Transfer your data to another service

6.3 Deletion

  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Note: Some information may be retained for legal compliance

6.4 Communication Preferences

  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Manage notification settings
  • Unsubscribe from newsletters

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analyze website usage and performance
  • Provide personalized content and recommendations
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud

You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling cookies may affect functionality.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce our agreements

Specific retention periods:

  • Account Information: Until account deletion
  • Order History: 7 years for tax and legal compliance
  • Prescription Data: As required by pharmaceutical regulations
  • Analytics Data: Anonymized and retained indefinitely

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than Ethiopia. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information during international transfers.

10. Children's Privacy

PharmaCare is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child, we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on our website
  • Sending email notifications to registered users
  • Displaying prominent notices on our platform

Your continued use of our services after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
