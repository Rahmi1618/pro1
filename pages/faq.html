All Frequently Asked Questions

Account & Registration

How do I create an account?

To create an account, click the "Login" button in the top navigation, then select "Create Account". Fill in your personal details including name, email, phone number, and create a secure password. You'll receive a confirmation email to verify your account.

I forgot my password. How can I reset it?

On the login page, click "Forgot Password" and enter your email address. We'll send you a password reset link. Click the link in the email and follow the instructions to create a new password.

Can I change my email address?

Yes, you can update your email address in your account settings. Go to your profile, click "Edit Profile", and update your email. You'll need to verify the new email address.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard SSL encryption to protect your data. Your personal information is stored securely and never shared with third parties without your consent. We comply with all data protection regulations.

Ordering & Purchasing

How do I place an order?

Search for your medicine, compare prices across pharmacies, select your preferred pharmacy, add items to cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay using TeleBirr, bank transfer, or cash on delivery.

Can I order without a prescription?

You can order over-the-counter medicines without a prescription. However, prescription medicines require a valid prescription uploaded to our system or presented to the pharmacy.

Can I modify or cancel my order?

You can cancel orders that are in "Pending" or "Confirmed" status. Once an order is being processed or shipped, cancellation may not be possible. Contact the pharmacy directly for assistance.

What if a medicine is out of stock?

Our AI system will automatically suggest alternative pharmacies that have the medicine in stock, or recommend similar medicines with the same active ingredient.

Payments & Billing

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept TeleBirr (primary), bank transfers, major credit cards, and cash on delivery. All payments are processed securely with SSL encryption.

Are there any additional fees?

TeleBirr payments have no additional fees. Bank transfers may have small processing fees. Delivery fees vary by pharmacy and distance, typically ranging from ETB 10-50.

How do refunds work?

Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days for cancelled orders. For returned medicines (due to quality issues), refunds are processed after pharmacy verification.

Delivery & Shipping

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary by pharmacy and location. Most orders are delivered within 30-60 minutes in urban areas. Rural deliveries may take 2-4 hours.

Can I track my order?

Yes, you can track your order in real-time through the Orders page. You'll receive SMS notifications for status updates including confirmation, processing, shipping, and delivery.

What if I'm not home for delivery?

Our delivery personnel will call you before arrival. If you're not available, they can leave the medicine with a trusted neighbor or reschedule delivery for a convenient time.

Prescriptions

How do I upload a prescription?

On the Medicines page, click the "Upload Prescription" area and select an image (JPG, PNG) or PDF file of your prescription. Our AI system will automatically extract the medicine information.

What file formats are supported?

We support JPG, PNG, and PDF files up to 5MB in size. Make sure the prescription is clearly visible and readable for accurate AI processing.

How accurate is the AI prescription reading?

Our AI has 95%+ accuracy in reading prescriptions. However, we always recommend verifying the extracted information before placing your order. Pharmacists also verify prescriptions before dispensing.

Technical Support

The website is loading slowly. What should I do?

Try refreshing the page, clearing your browser cache, or switching to a different browser. If the problem persists, check your internet connection or contact our technical support.

How does the AI recommendation system work?

Our AI analyzes medicine availability, prices, pharmacy locations, and your preferences to recommend the best options. It considers factors like distance, price, stock levels, and pharmacy ratings.

Can I use PharmaCare on my mobile phone?

Yes, PharmaCare is fully responsive and works perfectly on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers. We're also developing a dedicated mobile app.
